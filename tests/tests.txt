# Tests

## Test 1 - Keep homepage focused without utility cards [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: homepage content hierarchy, removal of Send a Gist/Get Alerts/Join utility cards from homepage
Description: Verifies the homepage no longer shows the utility cards that belong to dedicated sections while preserving the main campus content.
Steps:
1. Open the FUNAI SPEAKER TV homepage
2. Scroll through the What's happening section
Expected: Send a Gist, Get Alerts, and Join/Join the conversation utility cards are not displayed on the homepage.

## Test 2 - Open the dedicated Gist action from the Gist tab
Viewport: mobile
Covers: Gist tab navigation, Send a Gist placement, anonymous styled gist submission entry point
Description: Verifies the Send a Gist action is available in the Gist section rather than the homepage.
Steps:
1. Open FUNAI SPEAKER TV
2. Tap Gist in the bottom navigation
3. Tap Send a Gist
Expected: The Gist section shows the Send a Gist action and opens the anonymous styled gist composer.

## Test 3 - Keep breaking-update alerts inside News

## Test 4 - Submit an anonymous styled gist without a story title
Viewport: mobile
Covers: gist style selector, body-only validation, POST /api/submissions, newsroom review queue

2. Tap Send a Gist
3. Choose HOT TEA
4. Enter a gist in the card editor
5. Tap Submit for review
Expected: A confirmation appears and the gist is submitted to the newsroom review queue as anonymous content without requiring a story title.

## Test 9 - Delete a newsroom story as an authenticated admin [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: Admin Dashboard story deletion, DELETE /api/admin/posts/:id authorization, cascade cleanup, durable deletion after reload
Description: Verifies an unlocked administrator can permanently delete a story from the Story library and the deleted story is no longer returned by the dashboard.
Setup:
1. Sign in to the Admin Dashboard with the configured admin password
2. Create a story titled "Delete this newsroom story"
Steps:
1. Open Newsroom / Story library
2. Open the story action menu for "Delete this newsroom story"
3. Choose Delete story
4. Confirm the permanent deletion
5. Refresh the Story library
Expected: A Story deleted confirmation appears, "Delete this newsroom story" is absent from the Story library, and its related comments/reactions/analytics are also removed.

## Test 10 - Display comment age from the stored creation timestamp
Viewport: mobile
Covers: comment creation, backend createdAt timestamp, dynamic relative-time rendering
Description: Verifies a newly posted comment is given a real creation timestamp and the public comment display uses that timestamp rather than permanently showing a hard-coded Just now label.
Steps:
1. Open a story and open its comments
2. Enter a valid display name if prompted
3. Submit a new comment
4. View the submitted comment
Expected: The new comment initially shows a relative time such as Just now, and its stored timestamp is available for later relative-time calculation such as 1m ago, 2h ago, or 1d ago instead of remaining permanently hard-coded.

## Test 11 - Present story posts as segmented media cards
Viewport: mobile
Covers: public story card visual hierarchy, category/time metadata, headline/excerpt segmentation, author block, like/comment/share controls, responsive post layout
Description: Verifies public stories are presented as structured media cards rather than a plain block of text while retaining the existing interactions.
Steps:
1. Open the News feed with at least one published story
2. Inspect the story card
3. Tap the story headline to open it
Expected: The card has a distinct media area, category/time metadata, prominent headline, readable excerpt, separated author information and clearly grouped interaction controls; tapping the headline still opens the story.

## Test 12 - Keep published story media and timestamps durable
Viewport: mobile
Covers: storage-backed story image resolution, persisted createdAt timestamps, relative publication time, image refresh fallback
Description: Verifies a published story continues to show its uploaded image and its publication age is calculated from the persisted creation timestamp.
Steps:
1. Open a published story that has a cover image
2. Note the image and publication age
3. Refresh the page and reopen the same story
Expected: The cover image is still visible and the publication label reflects the real elapsed time such as minutes ago, hours ago, or days ago rather than resetting to Just now.
